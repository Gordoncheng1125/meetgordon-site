About

We wanted something you could use tomorrow.

Not a theory for people to argue about. Something that fits into an ordinary day.

Why this exists

Neither extreme.

We can’t avoid relying on AI for answers. We also don’t want to spend our lives criticizing it, opposing it or refusing to use it. What we want to explore is how to use it more responsibly, more intelligently, and with a clearer head.

Team

Gordon Cheng

Founder and designer. Product designer in New York, from Taiwan, and a creative director before that. Hatch ’n’ Pip started as his MFA thesis in Interaction Design at the School of Visual Arts, out of a question about why people go to oracles and to AI for the same thing: permission to act.
