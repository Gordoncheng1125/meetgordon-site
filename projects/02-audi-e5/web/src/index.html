The Challenge

Challenge #1

A German Car That Needs a China Story

Audi stripped away the Four Rings — the most recognized luxury auto logo in the world — to create AUDI, a standalone brand exclusively for China.

Every other foreign brand told the same story: "We brought our world-class product to your market." That story stopped working.

The gap: AUDI needed a brand narrative authentically of China — not "adapted for" China.

The Solution

Don't Argue. Show.

Both challenges had the same root: you can't persuade someone with claims.

The solution: find a single image that answered both questions at once — something undeniably Chinese, that simultaneously proved why German engineering still matters.

My original concept:
The Panlong Ancient Road (盘龙古道) in Xinjiang — 36 km, 600+ hairpin turns, climbing from 3,000m to 4,200m.

A road that is undeniably Chinese. A road that demands exactly what German engineering was built for.

For Challenge #1

The China Story

Panlong is iconic Chinese landscape — raw, dramatic, unmistakably rooted in this land. The film didn't need to say "this is a China story." The road said it.

A German car earning its place on a road that would break anything less — that's not adaptation. That's belonging.

For Challenge #2

Why Choose German

600 turns of relentless, unforgiving physics. Altitude, hairpins, cliff edges. This is exactly where German engineering stops being a talking point and starts being a feeling.

The confidence you have at 4,200m when the road drops away and the car holds. No spec sheet can create that.

The Process

1. Research

China EV market landscape; consumer perception audit — what "premium" means to Chinese EV buyers in 2025; competitive storytelling analysis (BYD, NIO, Tesla).

2. Brand Strategy

Identified the storytelling gap: AUDI had a visual identity and slogan but not yet a story. Found the single creative image bridging German engineering and Chinese belonging.

3. Creative Development

Panlong Ancient Road TVC concept — from location scouting rationale to narrative arc to shot list. Communication materials extending the film's visual language.

4. Launch

September 16, 2025, Guangzhou. Film production, visual materials, dealer toolkit. 10,153 orders in 30 minutes — 338 per minute.

Takeaways

What Worked

• One image, two answers: The Panlong road simultaneously solved the China-story problem and the why-German problem

• Show, don't argue: The film never said "German quality." It showed a German car on a Chinese road, and let the audience feel the answer

• The product earned the story: 787 hp and 773 km range meant the TVC wasn't aspirational — it was documentary

What I Learned

"The best brand storytelling doesn't explain the product. It makes you feel why the product exists."

You can't tell someone their belief is wrong. You can only give them a new experience that makes them reconsider. The Panlong film didn't argue against BYD or NIO. It created a feeling that only this car, on this road, could produce.

Next time: Extend the concept into a series — different legendary Chinese roads, each revealing a different aspect of the car's character.
