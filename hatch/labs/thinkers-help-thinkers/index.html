Thinkers Help Thinkers

An online forum where people help each other with big decisions.

Post your question and other people can answer it. AI organizes the answers, links related ones and filters out what’s clearly off topic. An AI answer is an average, and that’s a fair place to start. This lab adds people who have been through it, and connects you to them. Gordon wanted this when he applied to study in New York and knew almost no one in his position.

Goals

  • Reach people who have no one like them to ask
  • Bring good answers and the right people together
  • Find out if this should exist at all

Activities

  • An open forum for big questions
  • AI that organizes, links and filters answers
  • Introductions between people facing the same decision

Outcomes

  • Nothing yet. This one could fail
