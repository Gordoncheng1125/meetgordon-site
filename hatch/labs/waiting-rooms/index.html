Waiting Rooms

The biggest decisions get made in plastic chairs. What if someone were listening, and nothing were kept?

Immigration courts. Unemployment offices. Hospital corridors. People who get the least from technology often face the hardest questions alone. We lent eggs that record nothing and connect to nothing. Pip just asks, and the egg forgets.

Goals

  • Reach people who would never buy this product
  • Find out whether a listener with no memory helps
  • Learn where Pip should never go

Activities

  • Free loaner eggs at the door
  • Trained volunteers within reach of every chair
  • Materials in eleven languages

Outcomes

  • Going well: 2,300 people borrowed an egg at two legal aid clinics
  • Going badly: in a hospital pilot, people began asking Pip whether to consent to surgery. We stopped after nine days
  • Paused while we decide if this should exist at all
