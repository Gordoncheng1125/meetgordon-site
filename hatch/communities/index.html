Communities

The Clutch

Small groups who meet in person, bring their eggs and answer out loud.

How to join

Bring a question you can share.

  1. Find a Clutch near you. 140 groups in 31 cities. Six to ten people each.
  2. Show up. You don’t need an egg. Someone will lend you one.
  3. Start your own. The host kit is free.
From the Clutch

Queens Night Shift

Nurses and drivers who meet at 7 a.m. after work. Their rule: no one speaks twice until everyone has spoken once.

Taipei Second Act

People over fifty starting something new. They’ve hatched 900 cards and kept all of them in one box.

First Gen Jersey City

Students whose parents can’t advise them on the choices they face.
