Vibe Working

Free workshops that teach people who have never coded to turn their own work into an AI workflow.

We teach in churches, community centers and neighborhood groups. People bring a task from their own work, leave with an AI assistant that does it, and learn to check what it did.

Goals

  • Teach non-programmers to give AI their chores and keep the decisions
  • Learn what people ask AI in real life
  • Keep it free for anyone who can’t pay

Activities

  • Hands-on sessions in Mandarin and English
  • We start with safety and what to protect before installing anything
  • A public skill card from every session
  • A workshop kit any community can run without us

Outcomes

  • Seven sessions in the first eight weeks, in a church, a design school and online
  • Invited by a New York City economic development program to teach its founders
  • Questions from the sessions go into how Pip asks
