The egg, the cards and the app

Three things you can buy or download.

The Egg

$89

A voice-first pet for creators and knowledge workers, about the size of a real egg. Hold it, say what’s on your mind, and put it back in your pocket. It asks at its own pace over a few hours.

  • Voice only, no feed and no chat log
  • Breaks one vague question into small ones
  • Hatches a card when you’re finished
  • One-step delete, on the device and our servers
Pre-order the egg

Answer Cards

$14 / pack of 20

The fold-out card the egg hatches, on paper. Three panels: My question. What came out when I talked. Our answer. Keep them in a drawer and read them in a year.

  • Printed from your own transcript
  • Use them with or without the egg
  • Blank packs for classrooms
Order cards

Pip for Phone

Free

Your hatched answers, sorted by what you kept coming back to. Conversations only happen on the egg.

  • Every word you said, sorted by theme
  • What Pip noticed, in plain language
  • Works offline
  • No account needed for the first three hatches
