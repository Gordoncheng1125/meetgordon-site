Vibe Working

Free rooms where people who have never touched code turn their own work into AI work, and stay in charge of it.

Most AI education is sold to people who already have a head start. Vibe Working goes to the church hall, the community center and the neighborhood group. People bring a chore from their real week. They leave with an assistant that does it, and a habit of checking what it did.

Goals

  • Teach non-programmers to hand work to AI without handing over the decision
  • Learn what people actually ask when nobody is grading them
  • Keep it free for anyone who can’t pay

Activities

  • Hands-on sessions in Mandarin and English
  • Safety comes first: what to protect before you install anything
  • A public skill card from every session
  • A workshop kit any community can run without us

Outcomes

  • Seven sessions in the first eight weeks, in a church, a design school and online
  • Invited by a New York City economic development program to teach its founders
  • Every question from the room feeds the way Pip asks
