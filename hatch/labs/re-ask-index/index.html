The Re-Ask Index

How many times do you ask before you hear yes? A public dataset about the moment people talk themselves into being convinced.

When an answer disappoints us, we rarely argue with it. We ask again, a bit differently, until it agrees. People have done this with oracles for three thousand years. Volunteers donate a count of their re-asks, never the content, and we publish everything.

Goals

  • Make re-asking visible to the person doing it
  • Give researchers open data on how conviction gets manufactured
  • Put a number on the printout: “You asked four times. You changed ‘should I’ to ‘can I.’”

Activities

  • Opt-in citizen science through the phone app
  • A two-year study with a university research partner
  • A free classroom PDF for high school teachers
  • Quarterly public data release

Outcomes

  • 41,000 donated sessions so far
  • People re-ask 3.1 times on average before they stop
  • 62% soften “should I” into “can I” by the last try
