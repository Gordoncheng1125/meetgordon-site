the AI that asks before it answers

Let your answer hatch.

A small talking egg with AI inside. Tell it what you’re stuck on, and it asks you small questions out loud over a few hours. When you’re done, it hatches a card with your answer, in your own words.

Drive the AI. Don’t let it drive you.

We bring AI our biggest decisions in our smallest sentences.

AI answers in seconds, before we know what we’re really asking. What matters most usually isn’t in the question yet. Hatch ’n’ Pip slows down that one moment, so the answer you act on is yours.

How it works

Most of your answer isn’t in your question.

Don’t take the answer from your first prompt. Watch Pip take one question, ask three small ones and crack a little with each answer.

Nothing you type leaves this page.
