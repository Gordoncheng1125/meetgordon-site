Services

Sometimes you want a person in the room.

Trained guides, no scripts, and the same rule as the egg: nobody tells you what to do.

What we offer

Hatch Hour

$60

One person, one question, sixty minutes with a guide over the phone or in person.

Question Clinic

$1,200 / day · free for public schools

A day in your classroom or community center. Students bring a real decision and leave with a card.

Big Decision Week

$480

Seven days for a move, a divorce or a career change. One daily question and two calls.

How it works

Three steps. All of them are yours.

  1. You bring a question. It can be messy. Messy is better.
  2. You talk. A guide or Pip asks. You answer out loud.
  3. You keep the card. Your words, in your order.

Case: a Queens senior class

Forty-one seniors, one Clinic, a week before college decisions. Eleven changed the question they were asking. Three changed their mind. Their counselor kept the blank cards.
