These four projects don’t make money, and some of them will fail. We post the results here either way.

What we believe

Fewer moments of handing over our judgment

Sooner or later we all hand some judgment to AI. We want those moments to be as few as possible. AI is good for sharpening ideas and testing them quickly, and the answer we want is one we make together. We believe in the process more than most AI products do, because the process is where people show what’s theirs.
